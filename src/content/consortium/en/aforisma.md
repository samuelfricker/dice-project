SOCIETA' COOPERATIVA A.FO.RI.S.MA. - ACLI FORMAZIONE RICERCA SERVIZI MANAGEMENT is an Italian cooperative society based in Pisa, dedicated to vocational education and training. The organization adapts digital inclusion strategies and cybersecurity curricula for the Italian context.

Their network of educators and adult learners ensures that the DICE E-Learning platform is rigorously tested and optimized for diverse learning needs, aligning with the project's focus on ethical AI and accessible digital skills.
