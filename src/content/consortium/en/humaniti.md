Humaniti AI Solututions EOOD is a Bulgaria-based organization specializing in the ethical application of Artificial Intelligence and advanced technology solutions. They bring critical expertise in developing accessible and responsible AI methodologies to the DICE consortium.

Their advanced expertise ensures that the vocational education and training materials on AI are cutting-edge and grounded in ethical considerations, promoting digital inclusion across European communities. They contribute directly to the design and implementation of the DICE E-learning platform's curriculum.
