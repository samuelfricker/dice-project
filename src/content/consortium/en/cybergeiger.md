cyberGEIGER GmbH is the Project Coordinator and primary applicant for the Digital Inclusion through Cybersecurity and Ethical AI (DICE) project. Based in Germany, cyberGEIGER brings extensive expertise in cybersecurity education, digital skills training, and technological innovation to the consortium.

As the lead organization, cyberGEIGER is responsible for the overall Project Management and Evaluation, along with the administrative coordination of the Erasmus+ grant. They coordinate the consortium's tasks and spearhead the development of the DICE E-learning platform. They create accessible, practical educational resources that empower vocational education and training (VET) learners across Europe to safely navigate the digital world.
