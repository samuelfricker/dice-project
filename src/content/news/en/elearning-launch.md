The DICE project consortium is excited to share progress on the development of our dedicated E-Learning platform! As we enter the final stages of content creation and technical testing, we are currently targeting an official launch in **June 2026**.

### Preparing for the Future of VET

Funded by the Erasmus+ Programme of the European Union, the DICE E-Learning platform will serve as a centralized hub offering specialized vocational education and training (VET) courses. Our upcoming modules are being designed to empower learners with critical digital skills, focusing specifically on:

- **Cybersecurity Basics & Best Practices:** Practical skills to navigate the digital world safely and protect personal and professional data.
- **Ethical Artificial Intelligence:** Deep dives into the implications, applications, and ethical considerations of using AI technologies in modern workplaces.

### Built for Inclusion

In alignment with our core theme of "Digital Inclusion," the platform is being developed with strict adherence to modern accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1 AA). We believe that high-quality education must be inclusive by design, ensuring that all resources are usable by everyone, regardless of their background or ability.

### What's Next?

Our technical and pedagogical teams are currently working together to finalize the curriculum and ensure a seamless user experience. We look forward to opening the doors to our [E-Learning gateway](/e-learning) soon.

Stay tuned for more updates as we approach our June launch date!
